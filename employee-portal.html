🏢 Company Overview Learn about Sonoran Outdoor Equipment and its locations.

Sonoran Outdoor Equipment is an Arizona-based retailer specializing in hiking, camping, climbing, and outdoor recreation equipment. The company relies on a secure and dependable enterprise network to support employees, customers, inventory systems, sales operations, and communication between locations.

The company operates three locations across Arizona. The Phoenix Headquarters provides centralized business and technology services, including the company’s Internet connection. The Tucson Distribution Center supports inventory and shipping operations, while the Flagstaff Sales Retail Store serves customers in northern Arizona.

Headquarters

Corporate leadership, Information Technology, finance, human resources, and centralized Internet services.

Distribution Center

Sales Retail Store

Flagstaff, Arizona

Customer sales, product demonstrations, point-of-sale services, and local inventory management.

📊 Business Needs Understand what the enterprise network must support.

Sonoran Outdoor Equipment depends on its network infrastructure to keep business operations running across all three locations.

  • Reliable communication between company locations
  • Secure access to business systems and company resources
  • Real-time inventory and warehouse management
  • Dependable retail and point-of-sale operations
  • Centralized Internet access and network services
  • Remote monitoring and management of network devices
  • Scalable infrastructure that supports future growth
  • Business continuity during equipment or connection failures
🎯 Network Goals Review the priorities guiding the network modernization project.

The Enterprise Infrastructure team is modernizing the company network to improve security, reliability, visibility, and scalability.

  • Provide IPv4 and IPv6 connectivity throughout the enterprise
  • Implement scalable dynamic routing between locations
  • Segment users and services with VLANs and access controls
  • Provide resilient gateways and redundant network services
  • Secure administrative access to infrastructure devices
  • Support centralized Internet access through Headquarters
  • Improve network monitoring and troubleshooting capabilities
  • Prepare the infrastructure for new departments and locations
🧑‍💻 Your Role as a Junior Network Engineer Learn what is expected of you on the infrastructure team.

You have joined Sonoran Outdoor Equipment as a Junior Network Engineer assigned to the Enterprise Infrastructure team. You will work under the direction of senior engineers to implement and validate approved network changes.

  • Review each Enterprise Change Request before implementation
  • Configure routers, switches, and enterprise network services
  • Follow approved addressing, naming, and security standards
  • Verify connectivity before and after every network change
  • Use show commands and testing tools to validate operation
  • Troubleshoot unexpected results without making unauthorized changes
  • Document completed work and record validation evidence
  • Escalate unresolved risks or outages to senior engineering staff
Your responsibility: Every course project represents work performed within a realistic enterprise environment. Accuracy, documentation, validation, and professional change-management practices are essential.
📋 Enterprise Change Request Process Follow the approved workflow for every network change.

Network changes must be planned, approved, implemented, tested, and documented. Each Enterprise Change Request follows the same structured process.

  1. 1

    Review the Business Need

    Understand the operational problem, technical requirement, or expansion need driving the change.

  2. 2

    Review the Approved Change

    Examine the scope, implementation requirements, affected devices, risks, and validation criteria.

  3. 3

    Record the Current State

    Complete required pre-change verification and document the condition of the network before making changes.

  4. 4

    Implement the Change

    Apply only the approved configurations and follow the required implementation sequence.

  5. 5

    Validate Network Operation

    Use connectivity tests, show commands, and technical evidence to confirm that the change works as intended.

  6. 6

    Document and Close the Change

    Save required evidence, update documentation, and complete the Enterprise Validation Assessment.
