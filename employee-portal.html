👤 Your Role as a Junior Network Engineer

Welcome to the Sonoran Outdoor Equipment Network Engineering team.

As a Junior Network Engineer, you are responsible for assisting with the implementation, testing, validation, and documentation of enterprise network changes.

Throughout your time with the Network Engineering Department, you will complete a series of Enterprise Change Requests (ECRs) that gradually expand and improve the company's network infrastructure. Each change request builds upon the previous implementation, reinforcing enterprise networking concepts while simulating the responsibilities of a professional network engineer.

As you complete each Enterprise Change Request, you are expected to:

  • Review the Enterprise Network Standards Guide before implementing changes.
  • Follow the implementation tasks provided in the Enterprise Change Request.
  • Verify your work by completing all required validation steps.
  • Document and submit your completed work according to the course requirements.

Your work helps ensure that employees, customers, and business operations remain connected through a secure, reliable, and scalable enterprise network.

📋 Enterprise Change Request (ECR) Process

Every change to the Sonoran Outdoor Equipment enterprise network follows a standardized change management process. Each Enterprise Change Request (ECR) describes the work to be completed, why the change is needed, and how success will be validated.

Throughout this course, each ECR builds upon the previous implementation. As a Junior Network Engineer, you are responsible for reviewing the requirements, implementing the requested changes, validating your work, and documenting the results.

Understanding the ECR Summary

  • Requested By: The department requesting the network change.
  • Assigned To: The engineer responsible for completing the work. In this course, that is you—the Junior Network Engineer.
  • Priority: Indicates the urgency of the requested change.
  • Change Type: Identifies the type of change being performed.
  • Implementation Window: Provides the estimated implementation effort and required completion date.
  • Business Impact: Describes the expected impact of the change on business operations.

Additional Resources

  • Lab Expectations and Process Guide (available in Canvas) explains how to complete, validate, and submit each Enterprise Change Request.
  • Enterprise Network Standards Guide provides the organization's networking standards and implementation requirements.
  • Enterprise IP Addressing Plan contains the approved IPv4 and IPv6 addressing information used throughout the enterprise network.
