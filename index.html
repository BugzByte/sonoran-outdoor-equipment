About Sonoran Outdoor Equipment

Built in Arizona for Life Outdoors

Founded in Arizona, Sonoran Outdoor Equipment specializes in outdoor recreation equipment, camping gear, hiking apparel, and adventure accessories.

The company has built a strong reputation for quality products, knowledgeable staff, and exceptional customer service while serving outdoor enthusiasts throughout the Southwest.

As Sonoran Outdoor Equipment continues to grow, the company is investing in modern technology, dependable infrastructure, and scalable business operations across all locations.
